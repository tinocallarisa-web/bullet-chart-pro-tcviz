WHAT TO CHECK

Edit mode: setup guide on top; close with X.
2. Format > Style template: pick Boardroom (dark), then Report theme.
3. Sort > Variance %; Accessibility > High contrast On, Minimum text size 10.
4. Colors > fx on Actual bar and on Target marker.
5. Pro without licence: Second target, Band 1 limit, Forecast, Small Multiples By or
Vertical: watermark + banner. With the plan: no watermark.
6. Click a bar: cross-filter, others dimmed; Ctrl+click; bookmarks restore it. Arrows/Enter/
Esc. allowInteractions false: no selection. Format mode: on-object panes.
