WHAT CHANGED IN 1.3.0.0
- Forecast marker and vertical orientation are now Pro, as the Terms of Use state
  (1.2.0.0 gave both to everyone).
- Small Multiples [Pro] card visible to Free users; before it was hidden.
- Free users trying a Pro feature get Power BI's own notifications (notifyFeatureBlocked,
  notifyLicenseRequired). No licensing UI is drawn.
- Licence states Active and Warning accepted; isLicenseUnsupportedEnv and
  isLicenseInfoAvailable honoured (no purchase prompt where the licence cannot be read).
- Narrow visuals keep the bar visible; small-multiples panels no longer overlap.
- Removed supportsOnObjectFormatting (declared, not implemented).

LICENCE
IVisualLicenseManager only: getAvailableServicePlans() against Plan ID
"bullet-chart-pro-tcviz". Requested once, never blocks rendering. Any failure stays Free
without prompts. No external servers or network calls; privileges is [].

FREE VS PRO
Free: Actual vs Target with automatic variance, qualitative bands, IBCS mode, horizontal
orientation, two reference lines, tooltips, drilldown, cross-filtering, bookmarks,
keyboard navigation, high contrast.
Pro: Small Multiples, Forecast marker, vertical orientation.
