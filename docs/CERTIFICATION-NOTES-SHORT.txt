WHY THIS SUBMISSION
Replaces 1.3.0.0: conditional formatting on the bar colour, panel title colour, purchase
notice fixed, watermarked Pro preview, new brand icon.

PLEASE READ FIRST - PRO PREVIEW
Without a licence, IN EDIT MODE ONLY, Pro features render working under a "Pro preview"
watermark, which the guidelines allow for paid features. Intentional, not missing gating. In reading view, before the licence resolves, and where licences cannot be read
(Publish to Web, embed, export) the FREE result is drawn with no watermark, so a published
report never uses an unpaid Pro feature.

TESTING - FREE (no active plan)
1. Category + Actual + Target: bullets with variance labels and qualitative bands.
2. Click a bar: cross-filtering. Right-click: context menu. Arrows move focus, Enter
   selects, Escape clears.
3. Colors > Actual bar > fx: a rule over the Actual measure colours every bar; a colour set by
   hand on one category overrides the rule there. Free feature.
4. In EDIT mode add Forecast, or a field to Small Multiples By, or Orientation = Vertical:
   they render under the watermark, a banner names the feature and after ~10 s Power BI's
   Upgrade bar appears. Switch to reading view: free result, no watermark, no notice.

TESTING - PRO (active plan "bullet-chart-pro-tcviz")
1. Small Multiples By: one panel per value, no watermark and no notice.
2. Forecast: triangle marker, also in tooltip and legend. Orientation = Vertical: columns.
3. Small Multiples > Panel title color: titles readable over any border colour.
