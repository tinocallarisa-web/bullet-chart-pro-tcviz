BULLET CHART PRO -- TIPS & HINTS (v1.3.1.0)

GETTING STARTED
1. Drag a category (region, product, KPI name) into Category.
2. Drag your measure into Actual (Real). The bullets render straight away.
3. Drag the goal into Target. Variance (absolute and %) is calculated by the visual. No DAX.
4. Qualitative bands are on by default. Set their thresholds in Format > Qualitative Bands.

FIELD WELLS
. Category ................... one bullet per value; hierarchies and drilldown
. Actual (Real) .............. the bar; respects the measure's format string
. Target ..................... vertical marker; drives the variance label
. Forecast [Pro] ............. triangle marker for the expected end-of-period value
. Small Multiples By [Pro] ... one panel per value of this field
. Tooltips ................... up to 10 extra measures in the tooltip

FREE VS PRO
. Free ... actual vs target with variance, bands, IBCS mode, horizontal orientation,
           two reference lines, a colour per category, tooltips, drilldown,
           cross-filtering, bookmarks, keyboard navigation, high contrast
. Pro .... Small Multiples, Forecast marker, vertical orientation
Without a licence, a Pro feature leaves the chart as it is and Power BI shows its
own notice with the option to buy.

FORMAT PANE
. General ................ orientation (Vertical is Pro), IBCS mode
. Colors ................. actual, target, forecast, variance, bands, colour per category
. Qualitative Bands ...... show, band 1 and band 2 thresholds (% of data maximum)
. Variance ............... show label, show %, green/red threshold
. Labels ................. category, actual value, target value, font size and family
. Layout ................. category column width %, values panel width %, scale axis
. Legend ................. show, top or bottom
. Reference Lines ........ two lines: show, value, label, colour
. Small Multiples [Pro] .. enable, columns, synced scale, titles, panel colours

TIPS
. Variance threshold: 0 for a plain above/below colour, or a tolerance such as -5.
. Bands are a % of the largest value in the data, not of the target.
. IBCS mode replaces your colours with the standard monochrome palette.
. Reference lines suit a company benchmark or last year's average.
. Small Multiples: Sync scale on to compare magnitudes, off to compare shapes.
. Negative values switch the scale to bipolar automatically.
. Keyboard: click the visual, arrows move, Enter selects, Escape clears.

TROUBLESHOOTING
. Values on the right disappeared ..... visual too narrow; the bar keeps 40% of the width
. Small Multiples By does nothing ..... Pro feature; with a licence check Enable is on
. No forecast triangle ................ Forecast is Pro; check the field is not blank
. Vertical shows horizontal bars ...... vertical is Pro
. Only part of the categories ......... the visual receives up to 2,000 categories
. Panels scroll instead of fitting .... each panel needs room for its rows; make it taller
