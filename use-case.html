The Business Problem

A European retail chain needed to monitor actual sales against monthly targets across 5 regions and 30 product categories — 150 KPIs in total. Their previous approach used a standard bar chart with a separate DAX measure for variance, which required constant maintenance and was hard to read at a glance.

They needed a single visual that could answer three questions immediately:

  • Is this region above or below target?
  • By how much, in absolute and percentage terms?
  • How does the actual value compare to the historical performance range?

The Solution

Bullet Chart Pro replaces three separate visuals (bar chart + KPI card + gauge) with a single, information-dense chart. No DAX required — variance is calculated automatically from the Actual and Target measures.

Result: The team reduced dashboard page count from 6 to 2, and time-to-insight dropped from ~45 seconds of scanning to under 5 seconds per region.

Drag your measures in and the visual immediately shows variance labels and performance bands — no extra configuration needed.

Step 2 — Configure bands

In the format pane under Qualitative Bands, set the thresholds as percentages of the maximum value. A common setup for sales:

  • Band 1 (poor): 0 – 60%
  • Band 2 (satisfactory): 60 – 80%
  • Band 3 (good): 80 – 100%+

Step 3 — Enable Small Multiples Pro

Drag a dimension (e.g. Region) into the Small Multiples By field. Under Small Multiples in the format pane, set the number of columns. Enable Sync scale across panels to compare regions on the same axis.

Key Features Used

  • Automatic variance Free — no DAX, just drag Actual and Target
  • Performance bands Free — visual zones for poor / satisfactory / good
  • Conditional formatting Free — highlight specific categories with custom bar colors
  • Reference lines Free — mark budget thresholds or historical averages
  • Small Multiples / Trellis Pro — one panel per region, synchronized scale
  • Drilldown Free — drill from region → country → store
  • IBCS mode Free — monochrome palette for formal reporting

Tips & Best Practices

  • Use Sync scale in Small Multiples when comparing panels — it keeps bars proportional and prevents misleading comparisons.
  • Set the variance threshold to your business tolerance (e.g. –5%) so the red/green coloring only fires when it truly matters.
  • Pair with a slicer on time period — Bullet Chart Pro respects slicer filters natively via supportsSynchronizingFilterState.
  • Use Report Tooltips to add a detail page that pops up on hover for any bar.
